Keep the conversation going

Say what you want to build
Explain the work in your own words. NoManos passes your request to your coding agent and reads back progress and results, so you can follow along without reading every line of output.
Change direction out loud
Forgot a detail? Changed your mind? Speak to add context, or ask the agent to stop and take a different approach. You don’t have to wait for the conversation to end.
Give permission by voice
With Claude Code, hear tool approval requests and answer “allow” or “deny” out loud. Other supported agents can ask whether to retry a blocked action; approval behavior depends on the agent and its settings.
Keep your music on
Your music lowers while you or NoManos speak, then returns to its usual volume. Keep listening without reaching for the volume control.
Local speech, your choice
The default speech recognition and voice run on your Mac. Your coding agent still sends prompts and relevant code to its provider. Optional cloud voices send text to the voice service you choose.

Use the coding agents you know

Work in your own projects with Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity, or OpenCode. NoManos adds a voice interface to the coding tools you already use.

Claude Code claude

Add instructions during a running turn and answer live tool approvals by voice.

Antigravity agy

Talk through tasks in your project and hear progress as the agent works.

Codex CLI codex

Give coding tasks by voice and hear the results from your Codex sessions.

OpenCode opencode

Use your configured OpenCode models with spoken requests and responses.

Requires installed, signed-in command-line tools. Claude Code is also required for NoManos’s voice orchestration, even when another agent does the coding. Agent subscriptions and usage are separate.
