How it feels

Get up from the chair

Remember when coding didn’t mean being glued to a desk? Stand by the window or go make some lunch. NoManos keeps listening, so when the fix comes to you, you can say it from across the room and your agent starts working on it.

When the work is finished you hear a short summary of what changed, which beats squinting through pages of output. And if you remember a detail halfway through, just mention it. The agent picks it up without waiting for its turn to end, and you can tell it to stop whenever it heads somewhere you didn’t mean.

When your agent wants permission to run something, NoManos asks you out loud and waits for your answer. Your music can keep playing too, because it dips while either of you is talking and comes back up afterwards.

Lowering other apps’ audio needs macOS 14.2 and permission to record system audio.

Your agent

Keep the agent you already trust

There’s nothing new to learn. NoManos works with several coding agent harnesses and passes your words straight to whichever one you already use.

It supports Antigravity, Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode, once they’re installed and logged in. If you already use a coding agent harness, you don’t need any extra software, and whatever you pay for your agent today stays between you and its provider.

Voice

Your voice stays on your Mac

The listening happens on your Mac. Speech recognition and the default voice run locally, while your agent sends prompts and code to its provider the way it always has. If you pick a cloud voice instead, the text it reads aloud goes to that service.

Price

Try it for a week

Your seven days start the first time you open NoManos. If you want to keep it after that, you pay once from inside the app and never see a subscription.

A single license covers up to five of your Macs. It activates online and then keeps working offline for as long as 30 days between checks, although an agent that runs in the cloud will still need the internet.
